# Emberlight Nights Uploader

Emberlight Nights Uploader is a private, single-operator desktop tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel **Emberlight Nights** to publish the channel's own long-form ambience videos.

## What the tool does

- Renders original ambience videos (illustrated scenes with procedurally generated soundscapes) on the operator's own computer.
- Uploads each finished video to the operator's own YouTube channel through the **YouTube Data API v3** (`youtube.upload` and `youtube` scopes), with the title, description, tags, thumbnail, playlist and privacy status prepared in advance.
- Sets the initial privacy status to *private*, so that every video is reviewed by the operator in YouTube Studio before it is scheduled or published.
- Reads back basic status information about the uploaded videos (processing state, privacy status) to keep a local publishing log.

## What the tool does not do

- It is not offered to the public; there is no sign-up, no third-party users and no hosted service.
- It does not read, download, modify or delete content from any channel other than the operator's own.
- It does not collect viewer data, comments or analytics about other people.
- It does not post comments, like videos, subscribe to channels or send messages.
